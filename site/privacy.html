Privacy

How we handle the information you share with us. This is a working notice while our European non-profit entity completes registration. It will be replaced by a full policy, with the registered entity name and a data controller, once that is in place.

What we collect

Today the only information we receive from you is what you choose to send us, by email or through the contact form. That is your name, your organization, how you are reaching out, and your message. We do not ask for anything more.

How the contact form works right now

The form on our contact page opens your own email client and addresses a message to us. It does not yet send data to a server we operate, so at this stage no submission is stored in a database of ours. When we move to a hosted form, we will update this notice first and explain where the data goes.

How we use it

We use what you send only to reply to you and to understand how we can help. We do not sell it, and we do not share it with third parties for their own use. We do not send marketing emails.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies, and does not run third-party analytics that profile you. Our fonts are self-hosted, so loading this site does not hand your visit to an outside font provider.

How long we keep it

We keep correspondence only as long as we need it to follow up with you, and remove it when it is no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to contact@develop-hta.com and we will respond.

Contact

Questions about this notice can go to contact@develop-hta.com. Develop-HTA is a European non-profit core with a Sub-Saharan consultancy arm. Registration is in progress, and the registered entity and data controller details will be added here once confirmed.
