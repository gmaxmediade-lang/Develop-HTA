An evidence engine for health decisions across Africa

Health Technology Assessment is how a health system decides which technologies are worth it, judged on safety, effectiveness, and real value against the alternatives. The global standards already exist. Our work is making them usable where ministries, hospitals, and funders actually make the call, so scarce budgets buy the most health.

Most health systems adopt technology faster than they can weigh it

New devices, diagnostics, and treatments arrive constantly, but the evidence on whether they fit the local setting, the budget, and the actual burden of disease rarely arrives with them. Without assessment, that call comes down to a sales pitch or a guess. With it, the same money buys more health, and the technologies that reach a patient are the ones that earned their place.

Without HTA

A purchase decided by the pitch

  • Adoption driven by a sales case, not the evidence.
  • No test against the local budget or burden of disease.
  • Scarce funds spent on technology that may not fit.
With HTA

A purchase decided by the evidence

  • Adoption weighed on safety, effectiveness, and value.
  • Judged for this setting, this budget, this burden.
  • The same money buys more health, and it is defensible.

Two people who kept meeting the same gap

Develop-HTA was started by Marina, a medical doctor, and Jani, a biomedical engineer, who met through the HTAi Interest Group on Developing Countries. One works at the bedside, one at the bench, and both kept seeing the same thing: good technology reaching African health systems with no way to judge whether it belonged there. Develop-HTA is their answer.
