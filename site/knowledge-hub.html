Knowledge Hub

Frameworks and evidence in plain language, drawn from the public standards we build on. The aim is a working reference for anyone weighing a health technology in a low-resource setting.

This hub is being built. It grows as our engagements complete, so entries are added when we have something real to share, not before.

HTA frameworks

Curated explainers, pointing to the primary sources.

These entries curate and explain existing public standards in plain language. They are not original research. Each points back to the source it summarises.

Explainer

What Health Technology Assessment is

A plain starting point. HTA is how a health system decides which technologies are worth it, using evidence on safety, effectiveness, and value. This entry walks through the idea without jargon and shows where it fits a real purchasing choice.

Curated6 min read
Standard

WHO prequalification of health products

The global benchmark for safe, quality-assured medicines and devices. This entry explains what prequalification checks, why it matters for procurement, and how to read a listing before you commit budget.

CuratedSource: WHO
Reference

WHO compendium of innovative health technologies for low-resource settings, 2024

A catalogue of technologies suited to constrained settings. This entry summarises how the compendium is structured and how to use it as a shortlist when scanning options for a specific need.

CuratedSource: WHO, 2024
Standard

PATH technical standards for health technologies

Technical guidance for the design and evaluation of technologies in the settings we serve. This entry explains what the standards cover and where they help align a device or diagnostic with real field conditions.

CuratedSource: PATH
