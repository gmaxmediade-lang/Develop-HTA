The problem we exist to solve

Most health systems adopt technology faster than they can evaluate it.

Every year, new medicines, devices and digital tools reach African health systems with little independent evidence on whether they work, whether they are safe, and whether they are worth the cost. Develop-HTA brings Health Technology Assessment, the discipline that answers those questions, to the systems that need it most.

How an assessment works

From an open question to a decision you can act on.

01

Question

A health system brings a real decision: adopt this medicine, device or diagnostic, or not.

02

Evidence

We gather the trials, real-world data and costs, and quality-check them against international standards.

03

Appraisal

Benefit, safety and cost are weighed for the local context: disease burden, infrastructure, budget.

04

Recommendation

A clear, transparent recommendation, its reasoning shown, to adopt, negotiate or decline with confidence.

Questions

The things people ask us first.

What is Health Technology Assessment?

A structured way of judging whether a medicine, device or diagnostic works, is safe, and is worth its cost, before a health system commits to it. It is the discipline the world’s best health systems use to spend well.

Are you a non-profit or a consultancy?

Both, by design. A European non-profit core holds the mission and the methods; a Sub-Saharan consultancy arm delivers assessments to the systems that need them. The model is being formalised now.

Who do you work with?

Health ministries, hospitals, funders and manufacturers who need an independent read on whether a technology fits a given system, its patients, its infrastructure and its budget.

Where are you active?

Our focus is Sub-Saharan Africa, with methods anchored in European and global HTA practice. We are pre-launch and building our first partnerships.
