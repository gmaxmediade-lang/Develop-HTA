How an assessment actually works.

Four steps from a question to a decision a health system can act on.

HTA that meets each partner where they are.

From a single hospital to a ministry of health, the work fits the decision in front of you.

Healthcare organizations

Hospitals, faith-based providers, and health NGOs.

  • Prioritise technology investments
  • Compare the alternatives
  • Strengthen grant and donor applications

Public institutions

Ministries of Health, insurance funds, emerging HTA units.

  • Inform coverage and procurement
  • Use HTA tools where none exist yet
  • Strengthen institutional capacity

Local health industry

Device and diagnostics makers, digital-health startups.

  • Prove value for money
  • Prepare HTA-informed dossiers
  • Align design with what payers expect
