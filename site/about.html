About us

A non-profit committed to making Health Technology Assessment accessible across Sub-Saharan Africa.

Mission and vision

Our mission is to make HTA accessible across Sub-Saharan Africa, through a hybrid model that pairs a non-profit core with a mission-driven consultancy.

Our vision is simple. To empower Africa through HTA. We hold to one belief above all, that global standards are only as good as their local application. The frameworks already exist. Our work is making them usable where formal HTA structures are still emerging.

How Develop-HTA began.

We are Marina, a medical doctor, and Jani, a biomedical engineer.

We met through the HTAi Interest Group on Developing Countries, brought together by a shared interest in Health Technology Assessment and its potential in low-resource settings.

What we found there was a common dream. To empower Africa through HTA.

We did not want to build another technical repository. We wanted a bridge, so that every medical technology reaching African soil is safe, effective, and sustainable. That intent still guides everything we do.

One mission, two divisions.

A non-profit core sets the standards and protects the public interest. A mission-driven consultancy delivers on the ground. Consultancy revenue sustains the mission, so the work lasts rather than depending on grants alone.

Deeper governance detail will follow here as the two entities complete registration.

Public Advisory Division

The non-profit core

Europe-based, dedicated to equitable access to HTA and to the public interest in evidence-informed health policy.

For ministries, public partners, and funders.

Consultancy Division

On the ground in Sub-Saharan Africa

Mission-driven HTA services, technical support, and capacity building delivered locally.

For hospitals, health organizations, and industry.

The values behind every assessment.

Value

Equitable access

HTA should serve every setting, not only the well-resourced ones.

Value

Public interest

Assessments answer to health outcomes, not to any single supplier.

Value

Local ownership

We build capacity so local teams can run assessments independently.

Value

Evidence over hype

Decisions rest on what the evidence shows, not on marketing claims.

Value

Honesty about proof

We say what we can and cannot yet demonstrate, and never fill gaps with invention.

Value

Transparency

We show our sources and our reasoning, so any decision we support can be checked.
