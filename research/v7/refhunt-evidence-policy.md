# Develop-HTA v7 — Reference Hunt: EVIDENCE / HEALTH-POLICY / RESEARCH-COMMUNICATION orgs

Date: 2026-08-13
Angle: the truest in-class siblings for an HTA / health-policy / evidence institute — orgs that render dense evidence with elite editorial typography, clean left-aligned grids, and dignified real photography. This is the category v6 SHOULD have cloned (it wrongly cloned Wellcome Leap, a science-funder).
Method: each site opened in a live browser and probed for real computed typography (font, size, weight, alignment, tracking), real photography (img/webp/background-image sources), and grid craft. No guessing from raw HTML.

## HEADLINE FINDING (fixes the v6 "centered ragged" crime directly)
Every single top-tier site in this niche sets its headlines **LEFT-aligned / `text-align:start`** — Commonwealth Fund, Health Foundation, KFF, Nuffield Trust, Cochrane, RAND all measured left. **NOT ONE uses centered giant headline stacks.** The v6 disease (big centered headlines shattering into ragged 1-2-word columns) is not something any in-niche leader does. The corrective is confirmed by the whole category, not just by taste: left-align display headlines to the grid; reserve center only for a short single line if ever.
Second shared trait: restrained weights and tight tracking on display type (light-to-semibold, negative letter-spacing), never heavy shouty grotesque. Third: modular card grids with one consistent image ratio, not decorative canvas art.

## RANKED SHORTLIST

### 1. Commonwealth Fund — https://www.commonwealthfund.org/  (ANCHOR CLONE TARGET)
- Niche-fit: **5/5** — US health-policy foundation publishing evidence for a better health system. Same register as Develop-HTA: serious, non-partisan, evidence-for-policy.
- Why elite (measured): hero display is **Berlingske Serif Text at 80px, weight 100 (ultralight), letter-spacing -2.4px, left-aligned**, with a light-serif H1 ("Affordable, quality health care. / For everyone.") at 48px weight 200, tracking -1.44px. This is a masterclass in large, calm, editorial serif display — authority through refinement, not size-shouting. Clean modular card grid below (consistent 3:2 / related-content ratios).
- Real photography: **Yes, well.** Real dignified people/clinical photos throughout (image styles named Advancing_Health_equity, Rittenhouse, Kona_Medical, patient/clinician scenes) at disciplined fixed ratios. Exactly the "documentary-clinical, no poverty-porn, no globe cliché" register the domain research calls for.
- Steal for v7: the **large ultralight serif hero, left-aligned, negative tracking**, as the ONE signature type moment; real-photo card grid at a single ratio; navy/white sober palette with a single accent.

### 2. The Health Foundation — https://www.health.org.uk/  (PHOTOGRAPHY + DATA ANCHOR)
- Niche-fit: **5/5** — UK health-improvement charity/institute; evidence, analysis, and data on health and care. Dead-center of Develop-HTA's world (independent evidence body speaking to policy).
- Why elite (measured): **MessinaSans throughout, H1 at 32px weight 300 (light), left-aligned**, subheads 24px weight 500 — a quiet, confident sans hierarchy with generous line-height. Disciplined and legible, never ragged.
- Real photography: **Yes, well — best documentary use of the set.** Hero uses a real background photo (`photo-father-and-daughter-arriving...`), a genuine human health-context scene, alongside real data plots (dotplot, data_snapshot) and iStock clinical images at a fixed 400x400 crop. Photography and charts coexist in one clean system.
- Steal for v7: the **light-weight sans hierarchy + left grid**, the **real documentary hero photo** (human-in-health-context, dignified, not staged), and the photo-card + data-card modules living together.

### 3. KFF (Kaiser Family Foundation) — https://www.kff.org/  (EVIDENCE-FURNITURE / CHART CREDIBILITY FLOOR)
- Niche-fit: **5/5** — the independent source for health-policy research, polling, and news. Institutionally the closest analog to an HTA evidence org.
- Why elite (measured): **Source Sans 3, headings weight 600, left-aligned (`start`)**, 38px section heads / 28px card heads with tight line-height — utterly disciplined, no decoration. Everything reads as sober empirical authority.
- Real photography: **No — chart-led.** All 15 hero/feature images are branded "Quick Fact" / chart PNGs, zero photography. This is the reference for how an evidence org looks premium and credible with NO photos — the charting + finding-card system carries it.
- Steal for v7: the **chart-as-content + finding-card discipline** and left-aligned sober hierarchy for the Knowledge Hub / evidence sections; use as the credibility floor where photography is absent (honest for a pre-asset 2-person org).

### 4. Nuffield Trust — https://www.nuffieldtrust.org.uk/  (SOBER THINK-TANK EDITORIAL)
- Niche-fit: **5/5** — independent UK health think tank; analysis and commentary for policy. Same species as Develop-HTA.
- Why elite (measured): **editorial pairing — Utopia-std serif body + Circular sans headings (34px weight 700), left-aligned.** The serif-body/sans-head mix reads as considered, journalistic, trustworthy. Consistent card grid with author attribution (headshots + dated cards).
- Real photography: **Partial** — leans on real author headshots + purposeful limited-palette illustrated topic cards rather than hero photography. Good model for author/expert-led credibility, weaker as a photography reference.
- Steal for v7: the **serif-body + sans-head editorial pairing** as an alternative to an all-sans system, and the author-attributed evidence-card grid (fits Develop-HTA's founder-led, expert-authored positioning).

### 5. Cochrane — https://www.cochrane.org/  (EVIDENCE-SYNTHESIS SIBLING / REGISTER FLOOR)
- Niche-fit: **5/5** — global evidence-synthesis / systematic-review body; "Statistics that save lives." Evidence synthesis is HTA's direct cousin, so the register is spot-on.
- Why elite (measured): **Source Sans Pro, H1 weight 700 with slight negative tracking, left-aligned**, one consistent 34.56px heading scale. Clean and unpretentious; "we work to improve health for all" hero.
- Real photography: **Minimal** — largely brand/graphic led on the homepage; not a photography reference.
- Steal for v7: the **plain-strong evidence-brand register** and the confident one-line mission hero; a safe sober fallback if a more expressive move risks reading unserious. (RAND Health — https://www.rand.org/health-care.html — is an honorable mention here: Monument sans heads + Suisse serif body, left-aligned, dry-institutional; same lesson, less warmth.)
