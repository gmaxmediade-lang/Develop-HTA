## Per-candidate craft notes

### 1. Center for Global Development — cgdev.org  (niche-fit 5/5, photography: strong)
Why elite: this is the TRUEST in-class sibling by mission — "independent research for global prosperity", a health/development POLICY institute, exactly Develop-HTA's genre (not a funder, not a moonshot science shop). Craft: content is systematically LEFT-aligned with generous breathing room, NO ragged narrow columns, hierarchy carried by type size + whitespace not colour. Featured stories use dignified documentary photography ("African doctor talking to a child in a remote area", "a teacher with her student, Tanzania") as the credibility anchor — real, not stock pastiche. Restrained accent (functional links, no decorative colour). Structure: mission-statement hero -> four featured article cards (image + headline + author + date) -> "Our Areas of Focus" modular topic grid -> "Latest Work" list.
Clone value: this is the IA + register + typographic-discipline backbone. It proves a 2-person-feeling sober institute reads premium via left-aligned type + a few dignified photos. Directly answers the owner's ragged-centered complaint (CGD never centres giant type).
Watch-out: understated, editorial — borrow Gavi's hero photography for the top-of-page warmth CGD's own hero lacks.

### 2. Gavi — gavi.org  (niche-fit 4/5, photography: elite)
Why elite: photography IS the design strategy, done at the top of the market. Hero = full-width documentary image ("a mother and her child in Burkina Faso during a malaria vaccination session") with an overlaid statement "The state of global immunisation" + two CTAs — emotional narrative before functional content. Type flows LEFT-aligned throughout (centred reserved for short hero statements only), readable measure, no ragged columns. Work section = modular 3-column grid pairing thumbnail photography with concise text. Palette is restrained white + selective navy/teal on links/CTAs, and the PHOTOGRAPHY does the colour storytelling, not decoration — which is exactly our accent-discipline law.
Clone value: the single best template for "bring the photos back done right" — a dignified African-health documentary hero + a clean photo/text work grid, in a navy/teal register that maps 1:1 onto Develop-HTA's brand. This is the photography-craft ceiling of the shortlist.
Watch-out: Gavi is a FUNDER/alliance, not a policy institute — borrow its photo hero + grid, keep CGD's institute IA/positioning so we don't drift into funder-brand territory again.

### 3. The Global Fund — theglobalfund.org  (niche-fit 4/5, photography: prominent)
Why elite: large square (800x800) photo tiles carry serious visual weight; human impact stories are NAMED and specific ("In Eswatini, an HIV Breakthrough Delivered", "Dr. Bintou and Fanta") — dignity through specificity, not generic suffering imagery. "Learn More" = clean 2-3 column equal-block image grid.
Clone value: a strong model for a photo-led "stories / impact" section with named human subjects — useful later when Develop-HTA has real field partners; for now, the honest "coming soon" tile fits this grid shape.
Watch-out: hero is CENTERED full-width — the exact geometry that produced v6's ragged-centered failure at large type. If borrowed, keep the measure short and single-line, or convert to Gavi's left-overlay.

### 4. Resolve to Save Lives — resolvetosavelives.org  (niche-fit 4/5, photography: strong)
Why elite: full-width photo hero + a crisp 3-step method grid ("Identify -> Co-create -> Scale up"), a where-we-work map, a card news feed, and an HONEST impact-stat band (countries, people protected) — a clean, legible public-health-action system with real documentary photos (Thai doctor with stethoscope, Nigerian lab training).
Clone value: the 3-step "how we work" grid + honest stat band are directly reusable modules for Develop-HTA's method/what-we-do section.
Watch-out: layouts are CENTERED-heavy, including centred headings — this is precisely the anti-pattern the owner flagged. Take the module STRUCTURE (3-step, stat band, map) but render it LEFT-aligned, not centred.

### 5. PATH — path.org  (niche-fit 3.5/5, photography: moderate)
Why relevant: PATH layers geometric SVG knockout shapes (circles, hexagons, diamonds) OVER photography as a rhythm/brand device, with generous whitespace and a restrained near-grayscale palette + strategic link colour. It is more design-forward than photo-centric.
Clone value: this is the ONLY sanctioned source for an "abstract geometric" accent — and it works BECAUSE it is subordinate to real photography, never a replacement for it (the exact inverse of v6's mistake). If Develop-HTA wants a brand-mark rhythm (circuit/network motif) it should appear PATH-style: small, over/beside real photos, never as the hero itself.
Watch-out: do not let the geometric device grow into the primary visual — that is how v6 failed.

## Recommendation for the v7 clone (this category)

Primary clone target: **CGD (cgdev.org)** for the institute IA, LEFT-aligned typographic discipline, and register — it is the truest in-niche sibling (a policy/evidence institute, not a funder) and it structurally cannot produce the ragged-centered failure.

Grafted craft layer: **Gavi (gavi.org)** for the photography — a dignified African-health documentary hero with a left-overlaid single-line statement + the 3-column photo/text work grid, in Gavi's navy/teal-accent restraint that maps onto our brand. Add **Resolve**'s left-rendered 3-step method + honest stat band as modules, and reserve **PATH**'s geometric SVG only as a small subordinate brand-rhythm accent over real photos.

Net: CGD institute backbone + Gavi photography hero/grid + Resolve method modules + PATH geometric whisper — every section left-aligned, one teal accent, real dignified photography as the primary visual, honest "coming soon" tiles where proof is absent.
