## RANKED SHORTLIST (best craft references to clone for Develop-HTA)

### 1. iDSI — International Decision Support Initiative — https://www.idsihealth.org  ★ TOP PICK
- **Why elite (craft):** The truest in-class sibling by BOTH niche and craft — a global-health evidence network (HTA for LMICs, the exact Develop-HTA world). Clean modular sections stacked vertically (hero video → featured news cards → resource blocks → six icon "What We Do" tiles), generous whitespace, responsive grid, teal/blue-green accent on white. Contemporary web standards, sober and sophisticated, not experimental.
- **Niche-fit: 5/5.** Same audience (governments, ministries of health, institutional stakeholders), same "22 countries engaged" evidence-for-policy positioning. This is the sibling the benchmark research already ranked #1.
- **Real photography: YES, and well.** Authentic documentary imagery of healthcare in developing regions ("African Women", health-system scenes) used prominently to humanize the work — dignified, in-context, NOT poverty-porn or globe cliché. Exactly the photography register v7 must restore.
- **Clone what:** the whole IA + section rhythm + dignified photo treatment + teal-on-white sobriety.

### 2. NICE — National Institute for Health and Care Excellence — https://www.nice.org.uk  ★ TYPOGRAPHY REFERENCE
- **Why elite (craft):** The gold-standard HTA body worldwide, and its published design system is genuinely top-1% disciplined. Headings 1-4 in **Lora Semibold (a serif)**, H5-6 + body in **Inter**; a restrained type scale (H1 49px/1.2 → body 18px/1.6); **66-character line-length limit**; **sentence case headings, no trailing periods**. Token-based colour. This is the exact antidote to v6's headline crime (giant centered ragged stacks) — a real institution enforces measure + left-aligned editorial serif hierarchy.
- **Palette is almost identical to Develop-HTA's own brand:** NICE Teal #228096 + NICE Blue #00436c + NICE Cream #ded5ca on white/black. Navy + one teal — same discipline v7 needs. Strong proof the DHTA palette is correctly in-niche.
- **Niche-fit: 5/5** (the definitional HTA institute).
- **Real photography: YES** — NICE marketing/campaign pages use real photography; the main portal is guidance-dense. Clone the TYPE SYSTEM and COLOUR DISCIPLINE, not the dense content-portal IA.

### 3. ICER — Institute for Clinical and Economic Review — https://icer.org
- **Why elite (craft):** Polished, competent institutional site. Left-aligned headlines, restrained blue palette (no trendy colour), card-based modular layout ("Ongoing Assessments", "Featured News", policy papers) on a clear grid. Good trust-furniture and scannable structure — a solid layout/IA reference for an evidence body.
- **Niche-fit: 4/5** (US value-assessment / HTA body; same genre, US-payer framing).
- **Real photography: WEAK** — minimal/placeholder-ish stock ("woman listening to news at a party"). Use for LAYOUT + trust structure, NOT for photo direction.

### 4. HAS — Haute Autorité de Santé (France) — https://www.has-sante.fr  ★ PHOTOGRAPHY REFERENCE
- **Why elite (craft):** Sober navy-anchored government health-authority aesthetic; left-aligned headings, white-space-dominant, modular card grid for news/publications. The standout is its **authentic documentary healthcare photography** — real medical consultations, healthcare workers, patient-care moments (human-centered, not corporate-sterile). Best reference for HOW to shoot/select dignified real photos.
- **Niche-fit: 3.5/5** (national health authority; broader remit than pure HTA, dense govt IA is the weakness).
- **Real photography: YES, exemplary** — the model for v7's photo register.

### 5. HITAP — Health Intervention and Technology Assessment Program (Thailand) — https://www.hitap.net/en  ★ STRUCTURAL SIBLING
- **Why relevant:** The closest MISSION sibling — HTA built for LMICs, the exact Develop-HTA thesis. Professional, restrained blue/teal palette, structured full-width nav, clear IA (Research / Resources / About Us). Institutional credibility.
- **Craft caveat:** conventional and **icon/vector-heavy, not photographic** — information-accessible over visually elite. NOT a craft/visual bar.
- **Niche-fit: 5/5** by mission; **craft ~2.5/5.**
- **Clone what:** its INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE and content model (how an LMIC-HTA program organizes Research/Resources/About), NOT its visual design.
