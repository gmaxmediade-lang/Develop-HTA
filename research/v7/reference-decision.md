## THE PICK (one line)
**PRIMARY clone target: Center for Global Development — https://www.cgdev.org**
**Secondary A (photography + hero): Gavi — https://www.gavi.org**
**Secondary B (elite type signature): Commonwealth Fund — https://www.commonwealthfund.org**
Tertiary content sources (IA + type mechanics, not visual clone): iDSI (https://www.idsihealth.org) and NICE (https://www.nice.org.uk via design-system.nice.org.uk).

## 4-line rationale
1. CGD is the truest in-class sibling that ALSO clears the craft bar: a genuine independent research/POLICY institute (not a funder, not a moonshot science shop), whose homepage is systematically LEFT-aligned editorial with an author+date+type publication-card system and documentary development-context photography, so it structurally cannot reproduce v6's centered-ragged headline crime.
2. Gavi is the photography ceiling and answers the owner's "bring the photos back" verbatim: a full-bleed dignified African maternal/child-health documentary hero with a LEFT-aligned short overlaid statement, a consistent-ratio photo+text work grid, and a horizontal impact-stat band, all in a navy/teal-for-interactive discipline that maps 1:1 onto Develop-HTA's brand.
3. Commonwealth Fund is the typography ceiling: a large light-weight left-aligned serif hero with negative tracking and a single-ratio real-photo card grid, the exact antidote to the giant-centered-ragged stack, borrowed only as the ONE signature type moment.
4. iDSI and NICE stay as content/mechanic sources, not visual clones: iDSI is the literal HTA-for-LMICs sibling (borrow its IA, metric-as-proof, toolkit-library model, but NOT its centered video hero + centered value-prop headings, which carry the same centering liability the owner rejected); NICE hands us the measured type discipline (66-character measure, sentence-case headings, no trailing periods) that mechanically prevents ragged headlines.

---

## Why CGD over iDSI as PRIMARY (the close call, documented)
iDSI ties CGD on niche (it IS HTA for LMICs, the literal Develop-HTA world) and was ranked #1 in the original benchmark. It stays a first-class tertiary source for IA and the metric-as-proof pattern. But it loses the PRIMARY slot on craft: its firsthand teardown shows a CENTERED YouTube-video hero with centered overlay text and centered value-proposition headings — the same centering geometry that produced v6's rejected ragged stacks. Cloning iDSI section-by-section would risk re-importing the exact fault the owner called "terrible." CGD delivers the same institute niche and IA with LEFT-aligned editorial discipline and superior card craft, so it is the safer AND higher-craft backbone. Benchmark Selection rule = truest sibling by niche AND craft; on the product of the two, CGD wins.

## Why NOT Gavi / Commonwealth Fund as PRIMARY
- Gavi is a funder/alliance, not a policy institute. Making it the structural backbone is exactly the genre-drift that turned v6 into a funder-brand clone. It is grafted for photography + hero + stat-band ONLY; CGD keeps the institute IA/positioning so we do not drift into funder territory again.
- Commonwealth Fund is craft 5 but its niche is US health-policy (less in-niche than CGD's global-development institute) and its hero is photo-free ultralight serif; as a backbone it would fight the owner's "photos back" directive. Borrowed as the type-signature layer, it is ideal.
