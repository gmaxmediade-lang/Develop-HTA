## 1. Our World in Data
- URL: https://ourworldindata.org
- Craft signature: the data-visualization IS the visual language. Metric-as-hero opening ("14,049 charts across 126 topics"), then a chart-tile grid where every tile is a real figure with a source-chip beneath it. No photograph anywhere, and it still reads premium and authoritative.
- Why it fits a serious HTA non-profit: this is the definitive proof that pure evidence, cleanly typeset, out-ranks photography for credibility with ministries and donors. It is the exact register Develop-HTA lives in (sober, empirical, globally trusted) and it directly answers our defining constraint: premium with a zero-photo budget.
- Adapts to navy/teal/green + medical/Africa: swap its palette for our ink-on-white + teal whisper; render our charts (disease-burden, access, cost-effectiveness, an Africa choropleth) in the one house chart style; the source-chip becomes our WHO/PATH citation chip under each figure, which is our honest substitute for social proof. The Africa map becomes the "photo" the hero doesn't have.
- Watch-out: OWiD reads like a data catalog/tool, not an institutional mission homepage. We keep its visual SYSTEM but wrap it in our iDSI institutional IA (hero -> what HTA is -> what we do -> hybrid model -> stakeholders), not its tool-index structure.

## 2. ODI Global
- URL: https://odi.org
- Craft signature: an auto-cycling insight-card hero. The freshest piece of evidence (headline + one-line standfirst + one link) rotates as the hero itself, anchored by a single geometric mark (the propeller). The newest thinking is the visual; no stock imagery needed.
- Why it fits a serious HTA non-profit: this is our closest living REGISTER twin. A global-development think tank talking to the same ministries and donors, whose own current content is African health-spending finance. It proves a text-and-evidence org looks premium with almost no photography, exactly our situation.
- Adapts to navy/teal/green + medical/Africa: the cycling hero cards carry our WHO/PATH-sourced framings and (later) our real briefs; the propeller-style anchor mark maps onto our single line-motif (ECG-into-baobab / circuit trace) in teal on white. This solves the hero without the amateur AI-globe.
- Watch-out: the auto-cycle needs real, rotating content to feel alive. At launch we have few pieces, so start with a small honest set (mission framings + first news), not a fake feed.

## 3. Wellcome Leap
- URL: https://wellcomeleap.org
- Craft signature: premium carried by TYPOGRAPHY alone. A big editorial thesis hero and "What if..." provocation headlines, black/white with one dark-red accent and a single animated wave motif, then a modular program card grid. Almost no photography, maximum confidence.
- Why it fits a serious HTA non-profit: it is the type-craft ceiling for this class and the antidote to iDSI's slightly utilitarian dryness. It shows how to make a mission organisation feel confident and premium purely through type scale, whitespace and one rationed accent, which is precisely our accent-discipline law.
- Adapts to navy/teal/green + medical/Africa: their one red accent becomes our one teal whisper; the animated wave becomes our single restrained line-motif (baobab / circuit / continent as negative space); the provocation-headline device gives our hero a confident plain-language thesis ("Making Health Technology Assessment work for Sub-Saharan Africa") instead of a timid italic subline.
- Watch-out: Wellcome Leap's tone leans slightly moonshot/science-funder flashy. Borrow the typographic confidence and accent restraint, dial the drama down to institute-sober.

## 4. Bruegel
- URL: https://www.bruegel.org
- Craft signature: a black-led monochrome system where ONE modernist display face carries the entire hierarchy, from a huge headline down to small meta, and publications render as a type-driven card grid where charts stand in for photos.
- Why it fits a serious HTA non-profit: dry, authoritative European economic-policy seriousness, the credibility register donors respect. It is also the cheapest and lowest-risk of the five to execute well, because hierarchy-by-type-only is exactly the "size + weight, not colour" discipline the iDSI teardown already mandates.
- Adapts to navy/teal/green + medical/Africa: run our Source Serif / IBM Plex heading face as the single hierarchy engine on white; publications and Knowledge-Hub cards become the type-driven grid; charts fill the photo slots. The teal whisper appears only on links and the one hairline.
- Watch-out: pure type discipline can read austere. Use Bruegel for the typographic backbone, borrow one warmth device (a motif divider or a tinted band) from elsewhere so it does not feel cold.

## 5. IFS (Institute for Fiscal Studies)
- URL: https://ifs.org.uk
- Craft signature: an animated count-up impact-stat band that makes data the visual centrepiece, plus interactive calculators that signal rigor, no photography required.
- Why it fits a serious HTA non-profit: an independent research institute whose whole trust engine is objectivity and evidence, matching our "confidence through clarity, not claims" posture. The stat band is the elevated version of the iDSI metric-as-proof module we already plan to build.
- Adapts to navy/teal/green + medical/Africa: the count-up band renders our HONEST figures only (WHO-sourced burden/access numbers WITH citation, or "2 founders, 1 mission"), never fabricated country/client counts. Numerals in tabular-nums, teal accent on the active figure. A later interactive cost-effectiveness or coverage tool would signal method rigor the way their calculators do.
- Watch-out: the stat band is a graft, not the spine. We have almost no numbers yet, so at launch this is one honest module, possibly omitted in v1, not the hero.

## RECOMMENDATION

Clone: **Our World in Data** (https://ourworldindata.org).

Why: it is the only reference that fully solves our defining constraint, premium with zero photography, by making its own data-visualization the entire visual language, and it sits in our exact evidence/donor register. It was independently surfaced by 3 of the 4 scouts, which is a real consensus signal, not a taste call. For a build whose credibility must come from evidence and clarity rather than proof we do not have, OWiD's "the data is the hero, every figure is sourced" system is the highest-leverage craft to copy. We keep our iDSI institutional IA and wrap OWiD's visual system inside it, so we get an institution homepage, not a data catalog.

Runner-up to graft a device from: **ODI Global** (https://odi.org). It is the tighter REGISTER and DOMAIN twin (dev think tank, African health finance, ministries/donors). Graft its auto-cycling insight-card hero + single geometric anchor mark for the top of our homepage, so the freshest evidence is the hero visual. Then borrow **Wellcome Leap**'s typographic confidence and one-accent restraint as the polish layer over the whole thing.

Net: OWiD system + ODI hero device + Wellcome type confidence, all rendered ink-on-white with the single teal whisper.
