## Top-3 recommendation for Develop-HTA

### 1. iDSI (idsihealth.org) — the sibling to clone for IA + tone
Same domain (HTA / evidence-informed priority-setting for LMICs), same 2-audience reality (technical policymakers + implementers), same sober navy register. Its sitemap is effectively ours. Adopt its metric-as-proof pattern (state real, modest numbers — or an honest "coming soon" — never fabricated proof) and its Resources-as-toolkit-library as the spine of our Knowledge Hub. Fits our brand's deep-navy base directly.

### 2. GiveWell (givewell.org) — the model for our PRE-ASSET stage
We have no photography, no client proof, pre-registration. GiveWell proves a near-photo-free, text-and-citation-forward site can read as authoritative and premium. This is the single most important fit for our constraint: credibility built from transparency, stats-as-graphics, and citation density instead of a photo budget. Pair its accent-only color discipline with our navy → teal → green so the gradient is the one functional whisper, not decorative paint.

### 3. Our World in Data (ourworldindata.org) — the Knowledge Hub geometry
Our HTA Frameworks / Success Stories / Experts / News hub is exactly a topic-page knowledge model. OWID shows how to lead with evidence, deemphasize the chrome, and furnish every asset with trust signals (source, date, license, read-time). We copy the GEOMETRY (topic pages + trust furniture + muted palette letting content color carry) and skip the advantage (their interactive chart engine).

Supporting pattern to steal from PATH: repeating geometric SVG motifs (circuit/tech lines, cross, baobab/Kilimanjaro/continent outline as flat marks) give visual rhythm with ZERO photography — the exact bridge from "amateur all-blue Wix draft" to a sober evidence-tier build on a non-profit budget.
