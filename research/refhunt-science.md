## 1. Wellcome Leap  — TOP PICK for our no-photo constraint
- URL: https://wellcomeleap.org  (verified live via WebFetch)
- Elite/custom signature: A health-funder site that reads premium on TYPOGRAPHY + graphics alone, almost no photography. Bold display headlines, black/white with a single dark-red accent, animated wave graphic. Studio-grade restraint, serious register (global-scale health funding, donor/institutional audience).
- CRAFT SIGNATURE to steal:
  - Hero is a big editorial thesis line ("A breakthrough engine for health. At global scale.") — pure type, no photo. Program teasers are provocation headlines ("What if...").
  - The whole body is a modular CARD GRID of programs, each a consistent block (title + funding figure + one line). This is exactly how an evidence org lists its work-streams/reports without needing imagery.
  - One functional accent (dark red) used sparingly on the mark + a moving wave graphic = brand-as-design-element without decoration.
- Register fit: Excellent. Sober, high-contrast, institutional; credible to ministries/donors. No award-flash.
- Adapt to Develop-HTA: Swap the dark-red accent for our teal/green over a navy/ink base. Hero = a typographic thesis on HTA/evidence for African health systems, no photo. Replace the "program cards" with an evidence/work-stream card grid (methods, country programs, publications). Use an abstract line/wave motif (data-line or contour) tinted teal→green instead of photography.

## 2. Think Global Health (Council on Foreign Relations) — data-viz + editorial register
- URL: https://www.thinkglobalhealth.org  (verified live via WebFetch; Awwwards Honorable Mention)
- Elite/custom signature: Editorial newsroom for global health, navigation-first (no photo hero banner), wordmark centered with a thesis tagline "Better health begins with ideas." Serious CFR-backed register with genuine interactive data.
- CRAFT SIGNATURE to steal:
  - "Data Focus" / "Featured Data Points" blocks — big statistics presented as designed editorial objects, plus interactive maps (e.g. disease outbreak tracker with sized circles). This is how a text/evidence org earns a premium feel: the DATA is the hero visual, not stock photos.
  - Card-based modular grid where featured content spans wide columns and secondary items narrow — a clear editorial hierarchy.
- Register fit: Strong — reads like a credible think-tank, not a flashy award site. (Caveat: it does lean on documentary photography; steal its data-viz + stat-object treatment, not its photo dependence.)
- Adapt to Develop-HTA: Build "featured evidence point" stat blocks (big number + source line) in teal/green on navy. Use choropleth/Africa map data-viz as the hero visual device instead of photography. Keep the wide/narrow editorial card hierarchy.

## 3. Our World in Data — premium from pure evidence, zero photos
- URL: https://ourworldindata.org  (verified live via WebFetch)
- Elite/custom signature: The reference case for "text-and-data can look authoritative and clean with NO photography." Mission thesis hero ("Research and data to make progress against the world's largest problems") backed by a live count ("14,049 charts across 126 topics").
- CRAFT SIGNATURE to steal:
  - Hero proves scale with a live METRIC instead of an image — quantified credibility.
  - Chart-tile grid: every topic entry is a small chart thumbnail + label; charts ARE the visual system. Restrained, functional palette lets the data carry the page.
- Register fit: Excellent — trusted globally by researchers/ministries; sober, non-gimmicky. (Slightly utilitarian; we push the typographic craft further than they do.)
- Adapt to Develop-HTA: Adopt the "metric-as-hero" move (e.g. "X HTA assessments across Y African countries"). Chart-tile grid becomes our evidence/topic explorer, styled in navy/teal so it looks designed rather than plain. No photos needed.
