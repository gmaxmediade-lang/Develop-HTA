# Reference Teardown — iDSI (idsihealth.org)

Date: 2026-08-11
For: Develop-HTA (www.develop-hta.com) — small mission-driven global-health HTA non-profit, 2-person founding team, pre-registration, no photography/proof yet.
Purpose: extract the TRANSFERABLE construction (IA, evidence pattern, section geometry, restraint) from our closest living sibling. Copy the mechanics; skip the advantages.
Pages torn down: home, /resources/, /what-we-do/. Footer credit: "© Center for Global Development 2020" — iDSI is the HTA/priority-setting network run out of CGD Europe (London). It IS our domain sibling, but it sits inside a funded institute, so tag its scale advantages hard.

---

## 1. Why iDSI is the right skeleton to copy
Same domain (HTA / evidence-informed priority-setting for LMICs), same two-audience reality (technical policymakers + on-the-ground implementers), same sober navy-on-white register. Its sitemap is effectively ours. What we take: the IA blueprint, the metric-as-proof module, the Resources-as-toolkit-library spine, and the text-with-small-icon section rhythm that needs ZERO photography. What we leave: everything that only works because they have a 10-year real country network and a publication back-catalogue.

## 3. Hero pattern
iDSI hero:
- Value line: **"iDSI is more than just a network. It's a support system."** and a second anchor value block **"Better decisions. Better health."** (short, plain, benefit-first — no jargon in the biggest type).
- One CTA: **"Find Out More"** → strategy page. Single primary action, repeated verbatim down the page (their whole site uses "Find Out More" as the one CTA verb — consistency over cleverness).
- Background: an embedded YouTube thumbnail. **SKIP the video** — that's their asset. 
- Below the hero: two secondary featured cards (funding news, toolkit download) — a lightweight "what's new" shelf.

TRANSFER: (a) a SHORT declarative value headline in plain language (benefit, not mechanism) — replaces our current amateur italic subline "We empower the Sub-Saharan healthcare system by bridging global HTA standards with local realities"; keep that idea but shorten and de-italicise. (b) ONE primary CTA verb reused everywhere. (c) a two-tier value statement: emotional anchor ("Better decisions. Better health." analog) + one line of what/where.
SKIP: video-thumbnail hero and the generic AI globe. For our no-asset stage, the hero background should be flat navy → teal → green brand gradient with the flat SVG motif marks (cross / circuit lines / baobab-Kilimanjaro-continent as line art), NOT a photo and NOT a stock globe. That is the exact bridge from the Wix draft to a sober evidence-tier hero.

## 4. Evidence / Knowledge Hub geometry (the most reusable part)

### 4a. Metric-as-proof module (homepage)
Two large numbers, plainly stated, dated:
- **"22 countries engaged since 2013"**
- **"11 have made policy commitments towards implementing Health Technology Assessment"**
Plus a soft one inline elsewhere: "HTA Toolkit surpasses 100 downloads."

Construction: big numeral, one-line qualifier under it, a date anchor ("since 2013") to make it credible rather than vague. No chart, no animation needed.

TRANSFER the FORMAT. **Do NOT transfer the numbers — we have none.** For pre-registration we use the same visual module but fill it honestly:
- Either omit the stat band entirely in v1, OR
- Use it for non-fabricated facts (e.g. "2 founders, 1 mission", "HTAi Interest Group on Developing Countries — where we met", or a mission-scope figure sourced from WHO like burden/access numbers WITH the citation). Never invent countries-reached or clients.
This is the single most important discipline for our stage: same geometry, honest fill, "coming soon" over fake proof.

### 4b. Resources / Knowledge Hub layout (/resources/)
- A **card grid** of ~8 resource cards. Each card = numbered SVG icon + H5 title + 2-3 sentence description + "Find Out More" link. That's it. No photos.
- Real categories worth mirroring as our HTA-Frameworks taxonomy: **Capacity Building · Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning · Policy Briefs · HTA Toolkit · Ethics & Equity in Priority-Setting · Reference Case for Economic Evaluation · Cost-effectiveness Thresholds · Videos.**
- Below the grid: a **Knowledge Gateway** module = one explanatory block pointing to an external open-science library (F1000Research), listing content TYPES: "Technical Reports, Tools, Policy Documents, Briefing papers, Case Studies, How-to guides."
- Notably: **no filters, no tabs, no search facets on the page itself** — a flat, honest card grid. A lean org does NOT need a filtering engine.

TRANSFER: the flat icon+title+2-line-desc+one-link card grid IS our Knowledge Hub spine (HTA Frameworks, Success Stories, Experts, News all render as this same card). The content-TYPE vocabulary (Framework / Toolkit / Policy Brief / Case Study / How-to guide) is a ready-made taxonomy for our hub — adopt it. Use flat brand-gradient SVG icons per card as the photo substitute.
SKIP: the F1000Research external gateway (that's an institutional publishing partnership we don't have) and the depth of 8 fully-populated toolkits. Ship 3-4 real cards + honest "coming soon" cards styled identically so the grid reads full and intentional, never empty.

## 5. Section geometry vocabulary (how a lean, photo-free org fills a page)
From home + /what-we-do/, iDSI's repeating block grammar:
1. **Short intro paragraph** (org purpose, ≤3 sentences, first-person plural: "We share our skills and expertise in these six areas…").
2. **Metric band** (2 big numbers) — §4a.
3. **4 pillar blocks**: small icon + one-line claim ("Respond to country demand", "Encourage local ownership and capacity strengthening", "Ensure policies are evidence-informed, good value for money and fair").
4. **"What We Do" = 6 icon tiles**, each linking to a topic page. On the What-We-Do page itself these become **6 expandable blocks**: SVG icon + action headline + 2-3 sentence description + related links. Headlines are action/benefit phrased: *"Tackling healthcare challenges", "Smart purchasing for UHC", "Strengthening health systems and institutions".*
5. **Featured news** (3 dated post previews).
6. **Resources** (4 featured cards).
7. **Knowledge Gateway** closing module.

The whole page is: intro → proof → pillars → what-we-do tiles → news → resources → gateway. Every block is **small-icon + short-text**, consistent card sizing, generous whitespace. Photography is almost absent as a structural dependency.

TRANSFER: this exact block order and the "icon + ≤3 sentences + one link" unit as our page-building lego. Differentiate our sections by GRID (bento pillar row vs 2-col tiles vs 3-up news vs full-width gateway), not by paint. Action/benefit headline phrasing.
SKIP: the 6-way "related blog posts under every service" — that needs a content library. We attach founders' expertise / WHO-sourced framing instead.

## 6. Typography & scale
- Clean modern **sans-serif throughout** (single family, strong hierarchy). No display-serif, no personality font — sobriety signals credibility in this class.
- Hierarchy is carried by **size + weight**, not color. Big plain hero value line → section H2 → H5 card titles → body. Card titles sit at ~H5 (small, quiet), which keeps the grid calm.
- Body kept short: 2-3 sentence max blocks, high readability, accessibility-statement-in-footer discipline.

TRANSFER: one sans-serif family, hierarchy by size/weight, max ~4 type sizes, card titles small and quiet. Keep the accessibility posture (it's on-brand for a public-interest non-profit and cheap to honor).
SKIP: nothing here is advantage-dependent — this is fully copyable.

## 7. Color discipline
- **Navy + teal (logo) on white/neutral**, with image blocks providing the only visual breaks. Color is NOT decorative — it lives in the logo, links, and a restrained accent.
- Backgrounds are white/neutral; sections are separated by whitespace and the occasional tinted band, not by loud color fields.

TRANSFER: this maps 1:1 onto our brand (deep navy #123a5e → teal #1a9aa8 → green #5cb85c on clinical white). Discipline: the gradient is the ONE functional whisper — wordmark, key links, section-marker, active states, the metric numerals. NOT on headline text, NOT as full-bleed background paint. This is the direct antidote to the current "flat royal-blue everything" Wix draft: reduce blue to a functional accent on white, let whitespace do the work.
SKIP: n/a — but note iDSI leans on photo blocks for visual relief that we replace with flat SVG motif marks + tinted gradient dividers.

## 8. Motion vocabulary
- **Subtle to near-zero.** The site reads as static, content-forward: hover states on cards/links, a small image carousel on the Knowledge Gateway, an embedded video. No scroll-jacking, no parallax, no heavy reveals.
- Credibility in this class comes from restraint, not motion budget.

TRANSFER: keep motion to quiet reveals-on-scroll + hover lifts on cards + count-up on the (honest) metric numerals if we have any. That's the ceiling. A policy-institute audience reads motion-heavy as unserious.
SKIP: any award-site scroll cinematic — wrong register for this org and out of budget.

## 9. How a credible-but-lean org signals trust WITHOUT big media (the core lesson)
iDSI's trust is built from, in order of copyability for us:
1. **Plain dated metrics** ("since 2013") — format copyable, numbers not. → we substitute honest facts or a clean "coming soon".
2. **Named governance/backers** ("buy-in from Chinese, Thai, Norwegian and UK governments", CGD footer). → we substitute: the founders' real credentials (medical doctor + biomedical engineer), the HTAi Interest Group origin, and cited WHO/PATH source frameworks as our authority anchor.
3. **A real address + secretariat** in the footer (London, CGD Europe). → we show a real contact (contact@develop-hta.com), European non-profit core + SSA consultancy arm, and are honest about pre-registration ("registration in progress") rather than implying a corporate footprint.
4. **A structured resource library** with typed content. → we mirror the geometry with 3-4 real cards + identical "coming soon" cards.
5. **Consistency** — one CTA verb, one type system, one palette, predictable card sizing. Cheap, and it's 80% of why the site reads professional.

The takeaway for our pre-asset stage: iDSI proves an HTA org earns authority through **structure, plain language, cited backers, and relentless consistency** — not photography or motion. Our advantage-substitute stack = founder credentials + WHO/PATH-sourced framing + honest "coming soon" + flat brand-gradient SVG motifs + ruthless consistency.

## 10. Explicit SKIP list (advantage-dependent — do NOT clone)
- **"Where We Work" country network** and per-country pages (their 10-year, 12+ country reality). No fake map.
- **Real impact numbers** ("22 countries", "11 policy commitments", "100+ downloads"). Format yes, figures no — never fabricate.
- **Video hero** and stock/field **photography** dependency.
- **F1000Research Knowledge Gateway** external publishing partnership.
- **Named-government backers** and CGD institutional gravitas.
- A **live Blog** with a deep dated back-catalogue and "related posts" under every section.
- Separate **Team / Strategy / Network** sub-pages before we have the people/partners to fill them.

## 11. One-line build directive
Clone iDSI's IA spine + icon-and-text section grammar + metric-as-proof geometry + navy/teal-on-white restraint; fill every advantage slot honestly (founder credibility, WHO-sourced framing, flat SVG motifs, "coming soon"); reduce our brand blue from full-bleed paint to a single functional gradient whisper on white; keep motion near-zero.
