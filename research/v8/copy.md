Intro line (one sentence):
> Every assessment follows the same disciplined path, so the verdict can be trusted and repeated.

Step 01 — Evidence
> We gather the clinical trials, field data, and real costs for the technology and its alternatives.

Step 02 — Assessment
> We test what the evidence actually shows for this setting, this budget, and this burden of disease.

Step 03 — Appraisal
> We weigh the trade-offs against local priorities, with the people who will live with the decision.

> We deliver a clear, sourced verdict a ministry or hospital can act on and defend.

## 8. FAQ   (interactive accordion)   [SET; entries 4 and 5 FOUNDER-CONFIRM]

Section headline:
> Questions people ask us first.

Q1. What is Health Technology Assessment?   [SET]
> It is a structured way to judge whether a medical technology is worth adopting, weighing its safety,
> its effectiveness, and its value against the alternatives. It turns a purchasing decision into an
> evidence decision.

Q2. Who do you help?   [SET]
> Hospitals and health organizations choosing where to invest, public institutions shaping coverage
> and procurement, and health companies that need to show a technology delivers real value. If you are
> deciding whether a technology belongs in a Sub-Saharan African setting, the work is for you.

Q3. How do we start?   [SET]
> Tell us your question by email. We will point you to the evidence that already exists, or help you
> build the assessment that does not exist yet.

Q4. Do you replace our regulator or ministry?   [SET]
> No. We provide independent evidence and recommendations. The decision, and the authority, stay with
> you. Our job is to make sure that decision rests on sound evidence.

Q5. What does it cost?   [FOUNDER-CONFIRM]
> Every engagement is scoped to the question, so there is no fixed price list. Tell us what you are
> deciding and we will propose a way to work together.

Q6. Are you a registered organization?   [FOUNDER-CONFIRM]
> Develop-HTA is a non-profit currently completing its registration. We are open about where we are,
