## 1. What we are building (spec, restated)

A reference-tier website for **Develop-HTA** (www.develop-hta.com) — a small, mission-driven global-health non-profit making **Health Technology Assessment accessible across Sub-Saharan Africa**, via a hybrid model: a European non-profit core (public interest) + a Sub-Saharan consultancy arm (services, capacity building).

Audience is four-way and technical: **Ministries of Health / HTA units, hospitals & health NGOs, donors/funders, and medical-device makers.** They read **sobriety and evidence as credibility**, not motion or gloss.

Hard constraints that shape every direction: **2-person founding team, non-profit budget, pre-registration, and NO real photography or client proof yet.** Every proof-shaped element is an honest "coming soon" slot, never a fabrication. We are replacing an amateur all-blue Wix draft — the build must read as a health-policy institute, not a giant foundation.

## 2. Three design directions (each anchored to a NAMED reference, differentiated by GEOMETRY not paint)

All three share the SAME locked design system (navy/ink neutral ramp + one rationed teal-600 whisper, gradient only inside the mark/hairline, near-zero motion, flat SVG motif marks instead of photos). They differ by the **grid and composition** — the wireframe test: if you greyscale all three, the layouts are still clearly different.

### Direction A — Sober-Institutional / Evidence-Forward
**Anchor reference: iDSI (idsihealth.org)**, with GiveWell's photo-free credibility model and PATH's geometric-SVG rhythm as the fill.

- **Geometry:** a single centered authority spine. Measured text column, symmetric metric band (2-3 big honest numerals), symmetric 3-up / 4-up flat card grids for the Knowledge Hub, whitespace dividers between sections. Classic institutional stack — the "document library" feel. All-sans typography (hierarchy by size/weight).
- **Section grammar (from the iDSI teardown):** intro → metric/fact band → pillar row → What-we-do icon tiles → news → resources card grid → contact gateway. Every unit is small-icon + <=3 sentences + one link. Zero photography dependency.
- **Pros for this audience:** Ministries, HTA units and donors instantly read it as a credible sibling of iDSI/CGD/GiveWell — the exact register that wins technical trust. Cheapest to fill honestly; the flat card grid absorbs "coming soon" slots without looking empty. Direct antidote to the Wix draft (blue reduced to a functional whisper on white).
- **Cons:** Least distinctive at first glance — it looks like the category. Risks feeling dry to a device-maker or a warm donor who wants the human story. Leans hardest on copy quality to carry it.

### Direction B — Warm-Human / Bridge-and-Impact
**Anchor reference: Resolve to Save Lives (resolvetosavelives.org)** with Amref's Africa-framing-done-soberly.

- **Geometry:** editorial asymmetry. Offset two-column story blocks, large serif pull-headlines (Source Serif 4), the founders' story promoted to a wide featured band, and the line-art motif (baobab + Kilimanjaro + continent, medical cross) run as a **vertical spine element down the margin** rather than as icons in a grid. Magazine/editorial composition, not a symmetric stack.
- **Section grammar:** anchor tagline repeated (Amref pattern) → founder-led "our story" band → the bridge narrative (global standards <-> local realities) as an offset two-column → stakeholder self-sort → honest "coming soon" impact. Human warmth comes from layout generosity + serif voice, NOT from photos we don't have.
- **Pros for this audience:** Best for donors and the founder-credibility story (medical doctor + biomedical engineer, HTAi origin) — it makes the "bridge, not a repository" mission emotionally legible. Differentiates hardest from the sea of sterile HTA/NGO sites.
- **Cons:** Editorial asymmetry + serif reads slightly less "hard evidence" to a Ministry HTA unit or a device-maker doing due diligence. Warm layout with no real photography is harder to pull off — the emptiness shows more than in a flat card grid. Highest craft risk for a 2-person pre-asset stage.

### Direction C — Modern-Clean / Tech-Health
**Anchor reference: PATH (path.org)** for geometric-SVG rhythm, with Our World in Data's topic-page knowledge geometry.

- **Geometry:** modular **bento** composition. Tiles of varying size, geometric SVG motif tiles (circuit-board lines, medical cross, continent outline as flat marks — the gradient-"D" logo world) interspersed with content tiles. The Knowledge Hub becomes a **tiled topic-index** (HTA Frameworks / Success Stories / Experts / News as a dense modular grid). Denser packing than A, no editorial margin like B.
- **Section grammar:** bento hero (value tile + motif tile + one CTA) → modular pillar tiles → topic-index grid → typed resource tiles (Framework / Toolkit / Policy Brief / Case Study). Trust furniture on every asset (source, date, read-time) per OWID.
- **Pros for this audience:** Best fit for the device-makers / digital-health startups stakeholder and the "biomedical engineer" half of the founding story — signals technical modernity. The geometric-SVG motif is a genuine photo substitute, so the bento never looks like it is missing images.
- **Cons:** Bento is trendier and dates faster; a Ministry or a conservative donor can read "startup/product" over "public-interest institute." Densest to fill — more tiles = more honest "coming soon" slots to manage, and bento punishes thin content. Furthest from the sober iDSI sibling register that most wins the primary audience.

## 3. Recommendation

**Build Direction A (Sober-Institutional, anchored to iDSI), and steal PATH's geometric-SVG rhythm as the photo substitute and GiveWell's photo-free credibility model as the trust stack.**

Why A wins for THIS brand, audience, class and constraint: our primary decision-makers are Ministries, HTA units and donors, and in this category **sobriety is the credibility signal** — they trust what looks like iDSI/CGD/GiveWell, which is exactly our sibling set. A is also the **only direction that survives our pre-asset stage cleanly**: the flat card grid and honest metric/fact band absorb "coming soon" slots without ever looking empty or fabricated, and it needs zero photography. It is the most direct fix to the Wix draft (reduce full-bleed royal-blue to one functional teal whisper on white). B and C are not discarded — the recommended move is to build A as the spine and **borrow one lever from each**: PATH's geometric-SVG motif tiles for visual rhythm (a touch of C), and B's founder-story band on the About page so the human bridge still lands for donors. That gives us category-correct authority without the craft risk of a warm/asymmetric or trendy-bento full build on a 2-person budget.

One typography call to settle inside A (see open decisions): the iDSI teardown says **all-sans** signals rigor in this class; the brand note recommends a **Source Serif 4 + Source Sans 3** pairing. Recommendation: **all-sans (Source Sans 3) for A** to stay maximally institutional, and reserve the serif only if we ever move toward B's editorial warmth. This is an owner call.

**Where the honest "content-needed" slots go:** metric band (founder facts / WHO-cited scope only, never fabricated country/client numbers), Success Stories cards (all "coming soon"), Experts network (founders only + coming soon), any impact figure, testimonials/logos (none until real). All flagged in the global SLOT register in `brand-and-content.md`.

**Phase 2 — Deterministic gates + fresh-context reference-judge.**
Run selftest + discipline linter (must be green). THEN a fresh-context builder-not-judge pass: pixel-compare the home build to iDSI on IA spine, section grammar, restraint, and accent discipline — verdict against the named reference, not a rubric. Then Max's eye. Done != green hygiene gate.

**Phase 3 — Multi-page fan-out (reuse the same lego).**
What we do / About us (Mission-Vision + Our Story + founders + hybrid model; this is where B's founder-story band goes) / Knowledge Hub (HTA Frameworks / Success Stories / Experts / News as the flat typed-card grid — Framework / Toolkit / Policy Brief / Case Study taxonomy) / Contact. **Phase 4 — Device-split deploy.**
